# Track 2 brief: Trip Planner, built with Stripe

## The challenge (from the event page)
Build the challenger to Skyscanner and Omio: **search, compare, combine and pay for multi-leg journeys across Europe in one flow.**
- Payments must run on **Stripe**.
- The project is **auctioned when we pitch**. We can sell on the spot, or keep the IP if nobody buys.
- Prizes: Stripe Press books for the top 60 participants, **100,000 USD Stripe credits for the top 3 teams**.

## Side prizes we can stack

| --- | --- | --- |
| Best Sustainability (€1,000 cash) | City of Brussels | CO₂ per trip vs flying is already shown; push "greenest" and night trains |
| Best Business Model | Stripe (credits) | Booking fee is in; add Stripe Connect payouts per operator and/or a rail-pass subscription |

## Who judges
European Sleeper, SNCB, City of Brussels, Collibra. **Mostly rail people.** Trains first, night trains as the hero, honest about limits.
